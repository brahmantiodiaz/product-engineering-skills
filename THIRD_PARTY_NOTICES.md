# Third-party notices

This repository is a fork of mattpocock/skills; its own license is in `LICENSE`.

The gyrospec plugin ships its notices inside the plugin, so they travel with every installation: `skills/gyrospec/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` (adapted skills from mattpocock/skills and obra/superpowers), a `NOTICE.md` in each adapted skill folder, and `skills/gyrospec/gyro/scripts/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt` (packages bundled into `gyro.mjs`, generated by `packages/cli/scripts/bundle.mjs`).
