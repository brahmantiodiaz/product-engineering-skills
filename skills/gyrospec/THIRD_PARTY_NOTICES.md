The gyrospec plugin adapts skills from the two projects below. Each adapted skill folder also carries its own `NOTICE.md`, so a folder copied on its own keeps its notice. The packages bundled into `gyro/scripts/gyro.mjs` are listed with their licenses in `gyro/scripts/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt`.

## mattpocock/skills

Source: https://github.com/mattpocock/skills

`gyro-breakdown` adapts the `to-tickets` skill. The phase skills call skills from this project (`grilling`, `prototype`, `tdd`, `code-review`, `domain-modeling`, `codebase-design`), which are installed separately.
